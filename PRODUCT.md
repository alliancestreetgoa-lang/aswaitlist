# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

UK and internationally active business owners and investors researching UAE company structures, international tax considerations, banking, and relocation. They arrive evaluating whether Alliance Street is credible enough to trust with a real, high-stakes business decision (company formation, cross-border tax exposure, relocating themselves or a business). Traffic likely comes from social/ad campaigns tied to Alliance Street's existing marketing (Instagram/YouTube/LinkedIn) and word of mouth.

## Product Purpose

A priority-access waitlist landing page for Alliance Street Group's next live webinar on UAE company structures, tax, banking, and relocation. It exists to convert interested visitors into verified leads (name, work email, WhatsApp-confirmed phone) for a shortlisted, invite-only session, while offering an escape hatch to book a paid consultation directly for anyone who doesn't want to wait. Success is a completed, WhatsApp-verified signup; secondary success is a consultation booking via the "Would You Rather Not Wait?" CTA.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, direct, premium. This is a financial-services credibility play, not a startup pitch — confident condensed-caps typography, stark black/white/red palette, no soft-sell language, no filler reassurance copy. The page should read like it was built by an operator who works with serious money and serious risk (tax, banking, cross-border structuring), not by a marketing agency chasing clicks.

## Anti-references

Explicitly not the generic SaaS/startup look: no soft cream or beige surfaces, no pastel gradients, no oversized rounded corners, no gradient-fill CTA buttons, no playful consumer-fintech energy (bright colors, emoji, casual copy). Stay in the Swiss-dark register already established on alliancestreet.ae — near-black background, tight grotesk/condensed display type, red as a scarce, deliberate accent rather than a decorative gradient.

## Design Principles

- **Credibility over friendliness.** Every design decision should read as "serious operator," not "friendly startup." When in doubt, choose the more restrained, authoritative option.
- **One CTA, repeated.** The signup flow and the "book a consultation" escape hatch are the only two paths that matter; every section should point back toward one of them, never toward decorative distraction.
- **Verification is a feature, not friction.** The WhatsApp OTP step signals rigor (this operator doesn't take fake leads) — treat it as reinforcing trust, not as a UX cost to minimize away.
- **Red is scarce.** Red is the single accent that carries all emphasis (CTAs, stats, active states); if everything is red, nothing is.
- **Consistency with the parent brand.** This page inherits alliancestreet.ae's Swiss-dark visual system (near-black bg, bone text, grain texture, Anton/Barlow Condensed type) rather than inventing a new one — it should feel like the same company, not a spin-off microsite.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA: solid AA-level contrast for all text (including on the dark background and inside form fields), full keyboard operability for the multi-step form and FAQ accordion, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support for the GSAP scroll-reveal animations. No additional stated requirements beyond that baseline.
